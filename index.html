Who we are

An Egyptian consultancy for workforce, learning and performance

AWNAN designs and delivers consulting and training for employers, training providers, government bodies and donor‑funded programmes. We work in Arabic and English, in Egypt first and then across Africa and the Middle East.

Every assignment is led by a senior expert. Our leadership and associate experts have worked on USAID‑, EU‑ and GIZ‑funded programmes, written national occupational standards, run Lean programmes inside factories, digitised national TVET curricula and built HR systems for 40 companies.

ع و ن

Our name comes from the Arabic root for help and support. عوناً means “by way of support”: we stand beside our clients and help carry the work.

نقف إلى جانب عملائنا ونساعدهم على حمل العمل.
